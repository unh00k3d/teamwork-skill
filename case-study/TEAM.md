# Team: fit
Mission: Build a self-hosted, locally-run fitness tracker web app for a single user (desktop browser, data entered after the workout). Scope: AI-generated training programs that adapt to logged performance; fast keyboard-driven set/rep/weight logging against a program; body metrics with weight trend and body-fat % estimate; cardio logging; progress charts; and an interactive muscle heatmap — click a muscle group to see its exercises, its recent volume, and whether it is lagging. Self-hosted on the user's own machine, no mobile app, no cloud account.
